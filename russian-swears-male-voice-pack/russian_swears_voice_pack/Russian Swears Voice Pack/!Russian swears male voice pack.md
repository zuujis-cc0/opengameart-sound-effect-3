# Russian swears male voice pack
This is an asset pack of .ogg audio files of russian swear words spoken in a mostly calm male voice with a "well... shit." type of delivery.


## Contents
Includes these lines

Блять!
На хуй!
Охуеть!
Пизда!
Сука!
Бля!
Хуйня!
Пиздец!
Ебать!
Заебись!
Жопа!
Ебаный!
Пиздёж!
Fuck!

(They're common single word swears that mostly express "fuck, shit, damn" about an unfavorable situation. Only a handful could also serve double-use as insults, like the classic Cyka and Blyat, but the rest not so much.)

These are useful for game over screen sound bites, comedic character death sounds, ragdoll "pain" sounds, or for "access denied" moments in adventure games.
